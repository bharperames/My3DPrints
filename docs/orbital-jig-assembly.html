      What is drawn is what prints, and what was checked. The bodies come out of the 3MF the shop builds for this part, and the order, the path every piece travels and the hardware in each step come out of the same archive. Before the file was written, the generator moved each step's pieces along that path, half a millimeter at a time, against everything already in place — printed parts and the hardware fitted before them — and nothing could touch until it seated. A ✓ beside a step is that check, not this page's opinion.

      The bill of materials is the sum of the steps. If the BOM names something no step fits, or a step needs something the BOM does not name, the build fails rather than the bench. Inserts are listed where they are melted in; the board and its glue are not on the list.

      Fits are graded on the shop's PETG table for the P2S and a 0.4 mm nozzle, drawn hole minus pin on the diameter: press +0.10, slip +0.30, running +0.40. A printed hole comes out small, so a hole drawn at the pin's size jams. Each size is measured off the built part — a section loop, or a ray across a slot — not read from the number it was drawn with.

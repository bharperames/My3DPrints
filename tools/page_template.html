File ledger

All 27 files, mapped to their design family. Genus is the count of tunnels passing clear through a closed solid — a ball is genus 0, a donut or ring genus 1, and each extra handle or helical slot adds one. It is computed from the mesh itself (Euler’s formula: vertices − edges + faces = 2 − 2·genus), so it fingerprints the interlock mechanism no matter how the surface is decorated: the passthrough ball’s 21 helical channels read out as genus 21, and the hourglass bodies’ slot counts as genus 8–19.

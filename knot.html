What is simulated and what is drawn. What you see is the real generated mesh — bores, thread, chamfers. What the solver feels is an exact convex decomposition of the same solid: a box minus a convex pocket is the union of the box on the outer side of each pocket face, each convex by construction, so nothing is rounded off and the 0.25 mm keying fit still means what it says. Every collider was checked to contain its mesh completely, so the simulation is a shade tighter than the part, never looser.

A unit is one rigid body. Each bolt is threaded eight turns through its own bar. Nothing in a motion that takes seconds separates those two, so the thread is not simulated — a unit is a bar with a head standing proud of it.
